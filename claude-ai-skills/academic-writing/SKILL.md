# Academic Writing Skill

This skill produces academic papers with a structured, pragmatic approach combining theoretical rigor with practical applicability.

## Core Philosophy: "Bez Iluzija"

Apply honest, balanced assessment throughout:
- Present capabilities AND limitations equally
- Avoid hype while acknowledging genuine potential
- Ground theoretical frameworks in practical examples
- Be transparent about methodology constraints

## Document Structure

Academic papers follow this hierarchy:

```
Title (Heading 1)
├── Abstract (150-250 words)
├── Keywords (5-7 terms)
├── Author Information
├── Introduction (Heading 2)
│   ├── Context and significance
│   ├── Research questions (numbered)
│   └── Paper structure overview
├── [Literature Review / Background] (Heading 2)
│   └── Subsections by theme (Heading 3)
├── [Methodology] (Heading 2)
│   ├── Case study justification
│   ├── Data sources
│   ├── Analysis framework
│   └── Limitations
├── [Case Study / Analysis] (Heading 2)
│   └── Component-based subsections (Heading 3)
├── Discussion (Heading 2)
│   ├── Findings synthesis
│   ├── Ethical/practical considerations
│   └── Future implications
├── Conclusion (Heading 2)
└── References (Heading 2)
```

## Writing Guidelines

### Research Questions
Formulate explicit, numbered research questions in the introduction:

```
This paper specifically addresses the following research questions:
1. Which fundamental [components] of [subject] are essential for [purpose]?
2. What is the current capability of [tools/methods] in [domain]?
3. How might [framework] effectively integrate [elements] to support [goal]?
4. What specific [challenges] arise when applying [approach], and how do they impact [outcomes]?
5. What are the broader consequences for [field] if [conditions] become widely adopted?
```

### Methodology Section
Always include:
1. **Case study justification**: Why this specific case demonstrates the phenomenon
2. **Data sources**: Literature review, technical documentation, tool evaluation, community resources
3. **Analysis framework**: Systematic breakdown of components being examined
4. **Limitations**: Explicitly state what the methodology cannot address

### AI Tool Transparency
Include a disclosure statement when AI tools assisted the research:

```
AI tools included in the procedure of working on this paper are [tool names]. 
My obligation is to state that AI tools are not used in drafting the original 
text but rather in working with the already published sources. Additionally, 
[tool] was used for grammar checking purposes.
```

### Prose Style
- Use active constructions where possible
- Balance technical precision with accessibility
- Integrate examples and case studies within theoretical discussions
- Avoid excessive hedging while maintaining appropriate scholarly caution

### Citations
Use APA 7th edition format. See `references/citation-guide.md` for patterns.

## Workflow

1. **Define scope**: Identify core research questions and case study
2. **Structure outline**: Map sections to research questions
3. **Draft methodology**: Justify approach and acknowledge limitations BEFORE analysis
4. **Build analysis**: Component-by-component examination with evidence
5. **Synthesize discussion**: Connect findings to broader implications
6. **Frame conclusions**: Summarize contributions without overstating significance

## Output Format

When creating academic papers, use the docx skill for final output. Apply:
- Heading 1 for title
- Heading 2 for main sections
- Heading 3 for subsections
- Normal style for body paragraphs
- List Paragraph for numbered/bulleted items

For detailed section templates, see `references/section-templates.md`.
